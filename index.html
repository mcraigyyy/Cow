🐮 House Rules & Scoring

  • +1 Cow: Spotting individual cows (+1 point to Herd).
  • +20 Big Herd: Too many cows to count individually (+20 points to Herd).
  • 🪅 Exotic Animal (+15): Spotting camels, llamas, alpacas, or ostriches (+15 points to Herd).
  • 🌊/🪦 Drown Opponent: Spotting a Dam or Cemetery allows you to drown all UNBANKED cows in an opponent's herd back to 0!
  • 🏡 Cattle Yard: Bank 20 cows from your unbanked herd safely into total score.
  • 🚛 Cattle Truck: Bank your ENTIRE unbanked herd safely into your total score!
